---
title: Tooltip
description: Brief and informative text that appears when an element is hovered or focused.
---

## Usage

### Content

Wrap the element the tooltip explains, and pass the text in the `content` prop. The tooltip opens 150ms after the pointer enters the element, and at once when the element gets the focus from the keyboard.

::code-preview
:tooltip-usage-example

#code
```vue
<KTooltip content="Downloads every transaction from the selected period as a CSV file.">
  <KButton label="Export" variant="outline" color="gray" :leading-icon="Download" />
</KTooltip>
```
::

The tooltip adds no element around yours: it listens on your element directly, and points screen readers to its text with `aria-describedby` while it is open. Other attributes given to `KTooltip`, like `data-testid`, go to your element too.

Your element must be able to receive the focus, so keyboard users can open the tooltip. Buttons, links and fields already do. Give `tabindex="0"` to anything else, like an icon or a badge.

### Text trigger

Use the `label` prop to explain a few words in a sentence. They get a dotted underline while the tooltip is open, and can be reached with :kbd{value="Tab"}.

::code-preview
Your :k-tooltip{label="available balance" content="Includes pending transactions that have not cleared yet."} is updated every hour.

#code
```vue
<p>
  Your
  <KTooltip label="available balance" content="Includes pending transactions that have not cleared yet." />
  is updated every hour.
</p>
```
::

### Compact

Set `compact` for a short label of one to three words, like the name of an icon button. Compact tooltips use inverted colors.

::code-preview
:tooltip-compact-example

#code
```vue
<KTooltip content="Copy account number" compact>
  <KButton label="Copy account number" variant="ghost" color="gray" :leading-icon="Copy" square />
</KTooltip>
<KTooltip content="Download statement" compact>
  <KButton label="Download statement" variant="ghost" color="gray" :leading-icon="FileDown" square />
</KTooltip>
```
::

### Side

Use the `side` prop to open the tooltip `top` (default), `right`, `bottom` or `left` of its element, and `side-offset` to change the 5px gap between them.

::code-preview
:tooltip-side-example

#code
```vue
<KTooltip content="Opens top" compact>
  <KButton label="Top" variant="outline" color="gray" />
</KTooltip>
<KTooltip content="Opens right" side="right" compact>
  <KButton label="Right" variant="outline" color="gray" />
</KTooltip>
```
::

When there is not enough room on its side, the tooltip opens on the other side. It also slides along its element to stay 15px away from the edges of the screen.

### Align

Use the `align` prop to line the tooltip up with the `start`, the `center` (default) or the `end` of its element, and `align-offset` to move it away from that start or end.

::code-preview
:tooltip-align-example

#code
```vue
<KTooltip content="Balances are refreshed every 15 minutes." align="start">
  <KButton label="Start" variant="outline" color="gray" />
</KTooltip>
```
::

### Gutter size

Use the `gutter-size` prop to change the space inside the tooltip: `sm`, `md` (default) or `lg`. Compact tooltips keep their own tight space.

::code-preview
:::div{class="flex flex-wrap items-center gap-6 text-sm"}
:k-tooltip{label="Small" gutter-size="sm" content="Refreshed every 15 minutes"} :k-tooltip{label="Medium" content="Refreshed every 15 minutes"} :k-tooltip{label="Large" gutter-size="lg" content="Refreshed every 15 minutes"}
:::

#code
```vue
<KTooltip label="Small" gutter-size="sm" content="Refreshed every 15 minutes" />
<KTooltip label="Medium" content="Refreshed every 15 minutes" />
<KTooltip label="Large" gutter-size="lg" content="Refreshed every 15 minutes" />
```
::

### Max width

Long text wraps at 300px. Use the `max-width` prop to change it, in pixels, or set it to `none` to keep the text on one line.

::code-preview
:::div{class="flex flex-wrap items-center gap-6 text-sm"}
:k-tooltip{label="300px" content="Transfers made after 6 PM arrive on the next business day."} :k-tooltip{label="200px" :max-width="200" content="Transfers made after 6 PM arrive on the next business day."} :k-tooltip{label="One line" max-width="none" content="Transfers made after 6 PM arrive on the next business day."}
:::

#code
```vue
<KTooltip label="300px" content="Transfers made after 6 PM arrive on the next business day." />
<KTooltip label="200px" :max-width="200" content="Transfers made after 6 PM arrive on the next business day." />
<KTooltip label="One line" max-width="none" content="Transfers made after 6 PM arrive on the next business day." />
```
::

### Open delay

Use the `open-delay` prop to change how long the pointer must stay on the element before the tooltip opens, in milliseconds. When the pointer comes back within 300ms of the tooltip closing, it opens again without waiting.

```vue
<KTooltip content="Balances are refreshed every 15 minutes." :open-delay="600">
  <KButton label="Refresh balances" variant="outline" color="gray" />
</KTooltip>
```

### Content slot

Use the `content` slot to put more than plain text in the tooltip.

::code-preview
:tooltip-content-slot-example

#code
```vue
<KTooltip label="available balance">
  <template #content>
    <span class="block font-medium">$4,280.15 available</span>
    <span class="block text-muted">$312.40 is still pending and has not cleared yet.</span>
  </template>
</KTooltip>
```
::

### Interactive

By default, the tooltip closes as soon as the pointer leaves its element. Set `interactive` when the tooltip holds something to click, like a link: it then stays open while the pointer travels from the element to the tooltip, and closes once the pointer strays from that path.

::code-preview
:tooltip-interactive-example

#code
```vue
<KTooltip interactive side="bottom">
  <KButton label="Fees" variant="soft" color="gray" />
  <template #content>
    Transfers between your accounts are free.
    <KTextLink label="See pricing" href="/pricing" />
  </template>
</KTooltip>
```
::

### Conditional tooltip

A tooltip without content never opens. Pass `undefined` to show it only in some cases, like explaining why a button is disabled.

::code-preview
:tooltip-conditional-example

#code
```vue
<KTooltip :content="isSyncing ? 'Exports are paused while your accounts are syncing.' : undefined">
  <KButton label="Export" :leading-icon="Download" :disabled="isSyncing" />
</KTooltip>
```
::

### Clicks right after opening

Pressing or clicking the element closes the tooltip. Set `prevent-unintentional-click-to-close` to keep it open when the click comes within 400ms of the tooltip opening, which is often a click the user had already started.

```vue
<KTooltip content="Balances are refreshed every 15 minutes." prevent-unintentional-click-to-close>
  <KButton label="Refresh balances" variant="outline" color="gray" />
</KTooltip>
```

### Open state

Use `v-model:open` to know whether the tooltip is open, or to open and close it from your code.

::code-preview
:tooltip-open-state-example

#code
```vue
<script setup lang="ts">
import { ref } from 'vue'

const isTooltipOpen = ref(false)
</script>

<template>
  <KTooltip v-model:open="isTooltipOpen" content="Balances are refreshed every 15 minutes.">
    <KButton label="Refresh balances" variant="outline" color="gray" />
  </KTooltip>
  <p>The tooltip is {{ isTooltipOpen ? 'open' : 'closed' }}.</p>
</template>
```
::

## Behavior

The tooltip closes when:

- the pointer leaves its element, unless the tooltip is `interactive`;
- the element is pressed, clicked or loses the focus;
- :kbd{value="Esc"} is pressed;
- a pointer goes down anywhere else;
- the page, or an area around the element, scrolls;
- another tooltip opens, since only one is shown at a time.

It does not open on touch screens, where there is no hover.

## Keyboard

| Key | Action |
| --- | --- |
| :kbd{value="Tab"} | Focusing the element opens the tooltip at once, and leaving it closes the tooltip. |
| :kbd{value="Esc"} | Closes the tooltip. |

## Customization

Use the `ui` prop to change the classes of the tooltip (`base`) and of the text trigger made by `label` (`trigger`), and `class` for the tooltip. Your classes always win: `rounded-2xl` replaces the default `rounded-lg` instead of being added to it.

::code-preview
:k-tooltip{label="Rounder" content="Balances are refreshed every 15 minutes." :ui='{"base":"rounded-2xl"}'}

#code
```vue
<KTooltip label="Rounder" content="Balances are refreshed every 15 minutes." :ui="{ base: 'rounded-2xl' }" />
```
::

## API

### Props

| Prop | Type | Default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `content` | `string` | |
| `label` | `string` | |
| `open` | `boolean` | `false` |
| `side` | `'top' \| 'right' \| 'bottom' \| 'left'` | `'top'` |
| `align` | `'start' \| 'center' \| 'end'` | `'center'` |
| `sideOffset` | `number` | `5` |
| `alignOffset` | `number` | `0` |
| `maxWidth` | `number \| 'none'` | `300` |
| `gutterSize` | `'sm' \| 'md' \| 'lg'` | `'md'` |
| `compact` | `boolean` | `false` |
| `openDelay` | `number` | `150` |
| `interactive` | `boolean` | `false` |
| `preventUnintentionalClickToClose` | `boolean` | `false` |
| `ui` | `{ base?, trigger? }` | |
| `class` | `string` | |

### Slots

| Slot | Description |
| --- | --- |
| `default` | The element the tooltip explains. Plain text gets the same dotted underline as `label`. |
| `content` | Replaces the `content` prop. |

### Emits

| Event | Payload |
| --- | --- |
| `update:open` | `boolean` |
